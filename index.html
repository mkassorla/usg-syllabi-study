Research Ethics, Fidelity Mandate & Faculty Privacy

1. Absolute Faculty Anonymity In accordance with institutional research ethics, all instructor names have been suppressed and nullified in all exports, statistical distributions, and analytical views.
2. Statutory Fidelity Mandate Section 2.8 mandates that public syllabus postings must be identical to official classroom distributions, guaranteeing high-fidelity empirical grounding.
3. Automated Baseline Subtraction Campus-wide default statements are algorithmically isolated and subtracted before custom instructor policies are screened, preventing false-positive inflation.
